Long-form listening and transcription model lifecycle — 2026-09-22

Implementation
- Existing playback preferences now retain independent listening completion state;
  cards observe progress, and Details offers manual completion/uncompletion.
- MediaPlayer completion marks genuine playback completion; replay restarts at zero.
- Details has a scroll-to-top FAB, model toolbar state, and model/retry bottom sheets.
- Application-owned TranscriptionSession retains one native Whisper context, serializes
  model operations, and applies monotonic foreground/background idle deadlines.
- SegmentRetries remains the durable suggestion store. Its atomic start gate prevents
  multiple retries or starting before a previous suggestion is reviewed.
- Existing service ownership, cancellation, comparison, acceptance and rejection remain.
- Background completion posts a notification linking to the note. Notification failure
  cannot invalidate a saved result. Permission is requested when starting a retry.
