From audio to something useful

Built around the way you listen.

01 / TRANSCRIBE

Let the recording become text.

Download a Whisper model, import audio, and transcribe on your device. Edit timestamped text or regenerate an individual segment.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Make room for another language.

Download ML Kit language packs for ten translation pairs. Read inline translations or select a phrase for a quick lookup.

03 / KEEP LISTENING

Take the audio with you.

Listen in the background, control playback from the notification, and return to your saved position. Export text or a complete note with audio.

A simple place to start.

  1. Build the app using the instructions in the project README.
  2. Choose a model in Models, then import your recording.
  3. Read, listen, edit and optionally download translation packs.

Model downloads need an internet connection. Speed and accuracy vary by device, recording and language. Automatic backup is not currently available; export important notes to a separate location.

Your data, explained

Local processing. Clear boundaries.

Transcription and built-in translation run on your device. Downloads, Google ML Kit diagnostics, and sharing with another app can involve network services. Know what stays local and what leaves.
